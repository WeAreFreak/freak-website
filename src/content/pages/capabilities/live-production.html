One shoot, every channel

Planned product by product, so nothing has to be reshot.

Hoover needed new film and photography for 15 vacuum cleaners. We found a London family home, cast the talent, built the sets and planned every product in a shot matrix, so the film crew and the stills team worked side by side.

Before filming we tested debris on every machine to find the cereal, coffee and crumbs that picked up cleanly on camera and reset quickly between takes. Five days later Hoover had product films for web and Amazon and more than 120 retouched lifestyle images, and sales rose 30%.

How a shoot runs

From brief to final asset.

    The idea, the look and a shot matrix of every asset the shoot must deliver.

  2. Pre production

    Casting, locations, crew, props, wardrobe and a schedule for every hour.

  3. Tests

    Product, lighting and practical tests, so the day runs without surprises.

  4. Shoot

    Film and stills teams working together, with your team on set.

  5. Post

    Edit, grade, retouch and sound, reviewed in rounds you can comment on.

  6. Deliver

    Every size and format for every channel, named and filed to your system.

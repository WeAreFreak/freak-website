Cookie policy

Last updated 13th Oct 2026

This policy explains how wearefreak.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies

The site doesn’t set any cookies. We don’t use analytics, advertising or social media tracking.

What we store on your device

If you turn on the sound, the site remembers the music you picked so it can play it again on your next visit. This choice is saved in your browser’s local storage, stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Other services

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address when the page loads, as described in Google’s own privacy policy. Google Fonts doesn’t set cookies on this site.

Changes

If we add anything that uses cookies, such as analytics, we’ll update this policy and ask for your consent before setting any cookie that isn’t essential.

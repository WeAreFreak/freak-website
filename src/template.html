Capabilities

Creative, data and AI, under one roof.

Freak is an independent, full service creative and marketing agency. We don't believe you can separate creative from data any more, so the work we make for brand, social and the digital shelf is tested and measured for what it does to revenue. We build our own AI tools too, so the data does more of the work.

Everything is made in house, in our own production studio for 3D, 2D, live production and interactive. Our head office is in London, with global hubs in Barcelona and Abu Dhabi, and we look after clients in more than 100 countries. We work through the line as one integrated team, and we fit around the way your team works.

FAQs

What clients ask us.

What does Freak do?

Freak is an independent creative, data and AI agency. We make campaigns, content and commerce for global brands, run Amazon accounts end to end and build our own AI tools, then measure what the work does for sales.

Where is Freak based?

Our head office and studio are in London, with hubs in Barcelona and Abu Dhabi. We work for clients in more than 100 countries.

Is Freak an Amazon agency?

Yes. We run Amazon end to end, from account setup and vendor terms to catalogue management, A+ content, Brand Stores, advertising including Amazon DSP and review moderation. See our Amazon agency page.

Does Freak have its own production studio?

Yes. Photography, video, green screen, podcast and editing suites are all in house, along with 3D, CGI, motion graphics and animation. Our live production team handles location shoots.

How does Freak use AI?

We build our own tools in Freak Lab, from image analysis on our neural marketing framework to Amazon copy and review moderation. We use AI freely behind the scenes and carefully in front of shoppers.

Do you work with brands outside the UK?

Yes. We transcreate one master campaign into 5 - 25 markets and run Amazon, social and creator programmes across Europe, North America, the Middle East and Australia.

How do I start a project?

Tell us about your brief through our contact page, or email sales@wearefreak.com.

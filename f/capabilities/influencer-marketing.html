Creator seeding

Seeding that gets posts, not just parcels.

Sending product is easy. Getting it posted is the job. Every creator moves through the same nine stages, and each one is logged, so nothing relies on someone's inbox.

  1. Source

    Brand engagers, inbound requests, platform search and our roster.

  2. Vet

    Audience, recency, brand work and content quality checked.

  3. Approve

    The client approves each creator and picks the products.

  4. Outreach

    A message in their language, then two follow ups.

  5. Opt in

    Terms, address and usage rights in one form.

  6. Send

    Shipped within three working days and tracked.

  7. Content

    One post within 14 days, tagged and labelled #ad.

  8. Results

    Views, engagement and quality scored.

  9. Roster

    Strong creators move up, others don't get reseeded.

Vetting

Doing it properly

Clear labels, clear rights.

  • Labelled as ads

    If a creator agrees to post in return for a product or a fee, it's an ad and has to say so. We follow the ASA and CMA influencer guide in the UK and the FTC's disclosure rules in the US, and check every post.

  • Rights agreed up front

    Usage rights are part of the opt in and every contract, so you know where content can run and for how long. Addresses are kept in a restricted file and deleted after delivery.

FAQs

Questions clients ask us.

What does an influencer marketing agency do?

An influencer marketing agency finds the right creators for a brand, agrees what they'll make and what they'll be paid, briefs them, checks the content is properly labelled and reports on the results. Freak also runs product seeding and gifting, so smaller creators can join without a fee.

What is the difference between seeding, gifting and a paid campaign?

A paid campaign pays creators a fee for agreed posts. Seeding sends products to creators who opt in to try them and post. Gifting is the PR version, often a designed gift box with a note and a brief. We run all three together, so the best seeded creators can move up to paid work.

How do you choose creators?

Every creator is vetted before the client sees them. We check their authentic audience score, how much of their audience is in your market, whether they've posted recently, any past brand work, their follower band and the quality of their content.

Do you work with micro influencers?

Yes. We set the follower band for each client and product, so a programme can focus on micro influencers. Seeding and gifting suit them well, because smaller creators can join without a fee and we can work with many at once.

How do you make sure posts are labelled correctly?

Creators agree to label posts as ads before anything ships, and every post is checked for the #ad label before it counts. We follow the ASA and CMA guidance in the UK and the FTC's rules in the US.

Can we use creator content in our own ads?

Yes, if the rights are agreed. We ask for usage rights in the opt in, and when a post performs well we request image rights and test it as a paid partnership ad.

Which markets do you run influencer work in?

Our head office is in London and we run programmes across the UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the US, Canada and Australia, with outreach written in each market's language.

How do you measure influencer results?

Each post is scored on views, engagement rate and content quality, and costed per post. Those scores decide who gets seeded again, who moves to a paid brief and who doesn't.

Languages

Arabic, English and the languages of the UAE's expat communities.

Most people living in the UAE are expats, so reaching the market often takes more than Arabic and English. We write for each audience in its own language, with native speakers on every word.

ArabicEmirati and Arab audiences across the GulfModern Standard Arabic or Gulf dialect to suit the channel, with right to left layouts designed properly, never mirrored from English
EnglishThe region's shared business languageLocal references, spelling and claims for each market

FAQs

Questions clients ask us.

Does Freak have an office in Abu Dhabi?

Yes. Abu Dhabi is one of our three hubs, alongside our head office and studio in London and our hub in Barcelona. The same team works across all three.

Do you work with brands across MENA and APAC?

Yes. From Abu Dhabi we work with brands in the UAE, across the Gulf and the wider MENA region, and in APAC, where our working day overlaps with Singapore. Our wider team serves clients in more than 100 countries.

Do you make content in Arabic?

Yes. We produce content in Arabic and English, with copy written and reviewed by native speakers and layouts built for right to left reading, not mirrored from English. We choose between Modern Standard Arabic and Gulf dialect for each channel and audience.

Which other languages do you produce for the UAE?

Malayalam, Kurdish and Tagalog, for the expat communities that make up most of the UAE's population. Each is written and checked by native speakers. A campaign that only runs in Arabic and English can miss a large share of the people it is meant to reach.

Can you film in Abu Dhabi?

Yes. We plan shoots in the UAE with local crews, locations and permits. Qualifying productions in Abu Dhabi can also apply for the Abu Dhabi Film Commission's production rebate.

Can Freak run Amazon in the UAE and Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Amazon.ae and Amazon.sa run on the same Seller Central and Vendor Central tools we manage in other marketplaces, so we bring the same listings, content, advertising and catalogue management to the region. Amazon's assistant, Alexa for Shopping, is now in beta in the UAE too, so listings here need to work for conversational search as well.

How does the Abu Dhabi hub work with London?

Abu Dhabi runs on Gulf time, so the working day overlaps with Europe and Asia. Strategy and client teams work in the region, and production uses our London studio or local crews, whichever suits the brief.

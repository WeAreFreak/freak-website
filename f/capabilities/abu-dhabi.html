One team, three hubs

London, Barcelona and Abu Dhabi, working as one.

Abu Dhabi sits between Europe and Asia. Our morning overlaps with the afternoon in Singapore, and our afternoon with the morning in London. Briefs move between our hubs without changing hands, and the people you meet are the people who make the work.

When a production needs a studio, it uses ours in London. When it needs the region's light and locations, we shoot here, and qualifying productions can apply for the Abu Dhabi Film Commission rebate.

FAQs

Questions clients ask us.

Does Freak have an office in Abu Dhabi?

Yes. Abu Dhabi is one of our three hubs, alongside our head office and studio in London and our hub in Barcelona. The same team works across all three.

Do you work with brands across the UAE and the Gulf?

Yes. We work with brands in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and across the Gulf, and our wider team serves clients in more than 100 countries.

Do you make content in Arabic?

Yes. We produce content in Arabic and English, with copy reviewed by native speakers and layouts built for right to left reading, not mirrored from English.

Can you film in Abu Dhabi?

Yes. We plan shoots in the UAE with local crews, locations and permits. Qualifying productions in Abu Dhabi can also apply for the Abu Dhabi Film Commission's production rebate.

Can Freak run Amazon in the UAE and Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Amazon.ae and Amazon.sa run on the same Seller Central and Vendor Central tools we manage in other marketplaces, so we bring the same listings, content, advertising and catalogue management to the region.

How does the Abu Dhabi hub work with London?

Abu Dhabi runs on Gulf time, so the working day overlaps with Europe and Asia. Strategy and client teams work in the region, and production uses our London studio or local crews, whichever suits the brief.

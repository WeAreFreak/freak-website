One shoot, every channel

Planned product by product, so nothing has to be reshot.

Hoover needed new film and photography for 15 vacuum cleaners. We found a London family home, cast the talent, built the sets and planned every product in a shot matrix, so the film crew and the stills team worked side by side.

Before filming we tested debris on every machine to find the cereal, coffee and crumbs that picked up cleanly on camera and reset quickly between takes. Five days later Hoover had product films for web and Amazon and more than 120 retouched lifestyle images, and sales rose 30%.

How a shoot runs

From brief to final asset.

  1. Treatment

    The idea, the look and a shot matrix of every asset the shoot must deliver.

  2. Pre production

    Casting, locations, crew, props, wardrobe and a schedule for every hour.

  3. Tests

    Product, lighting and practical tests, so the day runs without surprises.

  4. Shoot

    Film and stills teams working together, with your team on set.

  5. Post

    Edit, grade, retouch and sound, reviewed in rounds you can comment on.

  6. Deliver

    Every size and format for every channel, named and filed to your system.

FAQs

Questions clients ask us.

What is live production?

Live production is filming and photographing with real people, products and places, as opposed to 3D or animation. At Freak it covers campaign and TV films, social video, lifestyle and product photography and ecommerce content, from pre production through to the final edit.

Does Freak have its own studio?

Yes. Our London studio has a shooting space, green screen, a podcast suite and editing suites. When a brief needs a real home, a street or an event, we shoot on location with our own crew.

Can one shoot cover campaign, social and ecommerce?

Yes, and that is how we plan most of them. A shot matrix lists every asset for every channel against every product, so a single shoot delivers the campaign film, social cuts, lifestyle images and the full Amazon image stack.

How far ahead do we need to plan a shoot?

Allow a few weeks for casting, locations and props. We have done it faster when the moment demands it. New York Bakery's three Channel 4 idents had under a month of planning and one day to shoot.

Do you handle casting, locations and wardrobe?

Yes. Casting, location scouting, set design, props, wardrobe, hair and make up are all organised by our production team and costed up front.

Do you shoot outside the UK?

Yes. We shoot on location wherever the brief needs it, and our hubs in Barcelona and Abu Dhabi support productions in Europe and the Middle East.

What happens after the shoot?

Editing, colour grading, retouching, motion graphics and sound design happen in house. We deliver every asset in the sizes and formats each channel and retailer asks for.

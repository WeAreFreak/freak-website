Global transcreation

Your global transcreation partner, in over 100 countries a year.

Brands that sell everywhere need content that feels local everywhere. We are a global transcreation partner, serving over 100 countries every year with localised content, from campaign films and social posts to Amazon listings and retailer pages.

It's done by native speaking creatives in our own team, not a translation agency at the end of the chain. They rewrite the copy for how people talk and search in each market, and we version the on screen text, subtitles and voiceovers to match. Formats are cut for the platforms and retailers that matter in each country, and claims are checked for each market before anything goes live.

How transcreation runs

One master, made local in four steps.

The same team makes the master and the local versions, so the idea, the tone and the claims carry into every market.

  1. Master

    One campaign, film or listing made with every market in mind.

  2. Transcreate

    Our native speaking creatives rewrite it for how each market speaks, searches and shops.

  3. Version

    On screen text, subtitles, voiceovers and formats for each platform and retailer.

  4. Check

    Claims, compliance and brand voice reviewed in each market before it goes live.

FAQs

Questions clients ask us.

What does a content production agency do?

A content production agency plans and makes the photos, videos, motion graphics and social posts a brand publishes. Freak covers the whole job, from the idea and the script to casting, the shoot and editing. Then our native speaking creatives transcreate it for every market, and we version it for every channel.

What is UGC and why do brands use it?

UGC, or user generated content, is content made by real customers and creators in their own voice. It feels native in social feeds and on product pages, which is why it often outperforms polished ads. We produce it end to end, from finding the right creators to editing the final cuts.

Do you cast creators for UGC?

Yes. We cast creators to fit the product and the audience, vet their audience before anyone is approved and brief them with a script, the shots we need and the claims they can make.

Who owns the rights to UGC you produce?

We agree usage rights with every creator before anything is filmed, including where the content can run, for how long and whether it can be used in paid ads. Those terms are logged against each asset.

How much content can Freak produce?

We produce more than 20,000 assets a year for a single global brand, across campaigns, social, Amazon, retailer sites and websites. Across all our clients we localise content for over 100 countries every year.

What is transcreation?

Transcreation adapts content for a new market instead of translating it word for word. The idea, the tone and the claims stay the same, while the words, references and formats change to fit how people speak, search and shop in each country.

Can Freak localise content for different countries?

Yes. We are a global transcreation and content localisation partner, serving over 100 countries every year. Our in house native speaking creatives adapt the copy, on screen text, subtitles and voiceovers. A single campaign usually runs in 5 - 25 markets from one master.

Who does your transcreation?

Native speaking creatives in our own team. They write for your brand in their own language rather than translating it word for word, they know how people search and shop in their market and they work alongside the team that made the master, so the idea survives in every version.

Which transcreation services do you offer?

Transcreation for everything we make. Campaign films and key visuals, social posts and UGC, product video, Amazon listings, A+ content and Brand Stores, retailer pages and websites, with subtitles and voiceovers where they are needed.

How quickly can you turn content around?

Reactive work can move very fast. For Macmillan's testicular cancer campaign we went from brief to six finished posts in ten days, timed to Wimbledon.

Do you use AI to make UGC?

We use AI where it helps, such as editing, versioning and transcreation. We don't pass off AI generated people as real customers. Our research shows labelled AI content can cut engagement and conversion, and laws such as New York's synthetic performer rules now require disclosure.

Complete management

Everything Amazon, with one team.

  • Account setup

    Vendor Central or Seller Central, Brand Registry in every marketplace, FBA or FBM and the roles and permissions your team needs.

  • Terms negotiation

    We model proposed vendor terms against your P&L and prepare the case for your annual negotiation, so every change has a number on it.

  • Catalogue management

    New listings, variations, bundles, suppressions, bylines and compliance fields. We fix 95% of issues in less than 48 hours and chase Amazon until content is live.

  • Listings that rank

    Titles, bullet points and backend keywords built from search data, plus an image stack designed with our neural marketing framework.

    A+ and Premium A+ below the fold, and Brand Stores with sales event content and tools like product finders.

  • Advertising including DSP

    Sponsored Products, Brands and Display, plus Amazon DSP to reach shoppers on and off Amazon, with banners and video made in our studio.

  • Reviews and ratings

  • Forecasting and reporting

    Stock forecasting, P&L management and digital shelf scorecards, reported weekly against ordered revenue.

    One master listing, transcreated for each marketplace and checked by people who know how shoppers there search.

How we run an account

A cycle that keeps paying back.

Every account runs on the same continuous improvement cycle. It starts with the gaps worth the most money and repeats every quarter.

  1. Gap analysis

    Each market and ASIN scored against competitors, best practice and what shoppers expect.

  2. Prioritise

    Opportunities ranked by the revenue they could add, with quick wins first.

  3. Create and test

    New copy, images and video, tested before they go live.

  4. Upload and verify

    We upload, check it's live and raise cases with Amazon when it isn't.

  5. Track

    Conversion, rank and revenue tracked before, during and after.

  6. Review

    The scorecard shows what to refine next, and the cycle starts again.

FAQs

Questions clients ask us.

What does an Amazon agency do?

An Amazon agency runs some or all of a brand's Amazon business. At Freak that means the account itself, the catalogue, every listing image and line of copy, Brand Stores, advertising including Amazon DSP, review moderation and the reporting that ties it to revenue. Most clients hand us all of it, some start with one part.

Do you work with Vendor Central and Seller Central?

Yes, both. We set up new accounts, register brands in Brand Registry in each marketplace and manage day to day operations on either model, including FBA and FBM fulfilment.

Can Freak negotiate our vendor terms with Amazon?

Yes. We model the proposed terms against your P&L, build the case with your sales and stock data and support you through the annual negotiation, so you go in knowing what each change is worth.

Do you run Amazon DSP campaigns?

Yes. We plan and run Amazon DSP alongside Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display, and we design the banners and video in our own studio. Hoover and Dr Scholl's both run DSP with us.

How quickly can you fix catalogue problems?

We work through issues as they arrive and resolve more than 2,000 catalogue issues a year, 95% of them in less than 48 hours. A case left with Amazon support can take two weeks or more, so if content has not gone live within 48 hours we raise cases with Amazon until it has. One Dr Scholl's Brand Store problem that had been open for three months was fixed in 48 hours, adding $500k to revenue.

Which Amazon marketplaces do you cover?

We work across the US, Canada, the UK and the EU marketplaces including Germany, France, Italy and Spain. We work further afield too, with content transcreated for each market from one master.

How do you measure success on Amazon?

Ordered revenue first, then conversion rate, click through rate, search rank, return on ad spend and star rating. Every change is tested before, during and after it goes live.

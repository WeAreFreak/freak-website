FAQs

About the Resource Centre.

What is the Freak Resource Centre?

It's where Freak publishes what we learn from client work. White papers on Amazon and AI, guides to the rules brands advertise under and the stories behind projects like Mapping Glastonbury for the V&A.

Who writes the guides?

The people who do the work. Stuart Willison, who leads our strategy and data work, James Carr, our Director of Amazon Services, and Daisy Fairbairn, our Head of Production, write most of them, with help from our Amazon, production, creator and AI teams.

Can we use these guides with our own team?

Yes, please share them. If you want the thinking applied to your own category or account, get in touch and we'll walk your team through it.

Where can I read about Freak in the press?

The press section above links to our interviews and features in LBB, the V&A blog and Creative Boom. Our company profile is on LBB too.

Hoover

From live shoot to listing

Live ProductionE-commerceAmazonPhotographyFilm
The challenge

Hoover needed new photography and film for 15 of its vacuum cleaners, from cordless sticks and uprights to cylinders, a robot and a wet and dry floor washer. Its existing imagery felt dark and sterile, and didn't look like the homes of the 20 to 40-year-olds it wanted to reach. The brief asked for more colour, a lived-in feel and more dynamic angles.

The idea

We produced the shoot end to end: the location, casting, set design, props and wardrobe, a film crew and a stills team, all planned product by product in a shot matrix. Before filming we tested debris on every machine, to find the cereal, coffee and crumbs that picked up cleanly on camera and reset quickly between takes. Over five days in a London family home we shot product films for web and Amazon and more than 120 retouched lifestyle images.

Then we turned the shoot into e-commerce. For every product we wrote search-led titles and listing copy, and designed the image stack above the fold and the A+ content below it for desktop and mobile, then reformatted it all for Argos and other retailers. We're now taking the work into Amazon DSP advertising and retailer campaigns.
